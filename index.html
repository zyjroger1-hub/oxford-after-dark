THE 30-SECOND RULES / 入门规则

Three rules. One way out.

  1. Click a lit road.

    You move to the next junction. While travelling, queue one next turn.

    点高亮的相邻路口；途中可以提前选下一次转弯。
  2. Collect 3 gold clues.

    Touch the numbered diamonds. In Open Dossier, choose any 3 of 5.

    抵达金色菱形自动收集；开放档案模式可 5 选 3。
  3. Reach an open arrow.

    Clues unlock the exit. The watch catches you within 6m by road. Corners break its sight.

    线索收齐后出口解锁；追捕者沿路距离 6 米内可抓住你，转弯可断开视野。

⌂ Hide / H · shelter only · 6s stillness · 2 uses

◇ Decoy / D · junction only · sounds after 4s · 2 uses

↝ Sprint / S · junction only · 5s faster, footsteps heard within 200m · 2 uses

Space pauses everything. Dotted route hints ignore danger. A remembered enemy marker is labelled LAST SEEN. You have 6 minutes in a real night. Safe practice has no capture or deadline.

EXPLAIN / LAB

It searches. It doesn’t know.

Greedy follows the shortest route to its last observation, then patrols least recently checked public objectives. Search diffuses probability across neighbouring road junctions and removes visible, empty non-shelter junctions. Intercept uses observed heading and public goals to compare arrival times at upcoming junctions; without sight it scores candidate coverage and public goal junctions.

The AI never receives your hidden position, collected clues, click queue or future route. The sensor boundary is Game.sensor(); decision code is in src/ai.js. The distribution approximates junction occupancy; it is a heuristic, not a trained model.

Both characters travel at fictional game speeds: runner 4.4m/s, pursuer 4.05m/s. Two optional sprints last 5s at 6.6m/s and emit footsteps at the launch junction. The AI cannot distinguish them from decoys by sound. Sight ranges are 62 / 78 / 90m. Visibility uses road distance versus straight-line distance; buildings are visual context, not a precise 3D occlusion model.
