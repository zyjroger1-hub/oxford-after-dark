# Oxford After Dark — 2.0.1-beta.1

First public playable beta: a short pursuit game on cached Oxford OpenStreetMap geometry.

- Five-step safe practice and concise English/Chinese beginner rules.
- The Archive mission and Open Dossier (choose any 3 of 5 clues, either of 2 exits).
- Greedy, Search and Intercept pursuers limited to permitted observations.
- Shelters, delayed decoys and finite noisy sprints; optional shortest-route guidance.
- Outcome badges and a scrub-able replay of actual routes, sightings, beliefs, sounds and decision reasons.
- Zero runtime dependencies or paid APIs; play in a static browser or run a local Python/Node server.
- 41 core/regression tests and reproducible 1,200-match reports, with repeated-layout and sensing limitations stated.

Release hardening fixes invalid seed handling, keyboard event propagation, live Lantern marker motion and fallback decision records. The public bundle separates MIT code from ODbL map data and includes its losslessly compressed source and attribution.

This is a desktop-focused **beta**. Three authored arrangements, approximate line of sight, limited independent human testing and no full cross-browser/accessibility audit remain. See RELEASE_REVIEW.md, QA_V2.md and the experiment reports.
